# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Bookmark Search is a PowerToys Command Palette extension that searches your browser bookmarks.

## What it reads

- The bookmark files of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Brave on your PC (each profile's `Bookmarks` file), or the files you list in its settings.

## What it stores

- Its own settings (navigation mode and bookmark file paths), saved locally in the extension's app data folder.

## What it does not do

- It does not send any data over the network. The package does not request internet access.
- It does not collect analytics, telemetry or crash reports.
- It does not share data with anyone.

Bookmarks are read into memory only while the extension runs and are opened in your default browser when you choose one.
